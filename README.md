## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
VERDANT AI is a digital agricultural marketplace that connects farmers, FPOs, consumers, and bulk buyers directly, creating a transparent and efficient agricultural supply chain.

### Key Features

- **Direct Market Access:** Enables farmers to sell products directly to consumers and bulk buyers.
- **Fair Price Insights:** Promotes transparent pricing and better selling opportunities.
- **AI Demand Forecasting:** Predicts market demand to support better production planning.
- **Smart Route Optimization:** Improves delivery efficiency and reduces transportation costs.
- **Shared Delivery Network:** Allows nearby farmers to coordinate transportation.
- **Harvest-to-Market Connection:** Connects farmers with potential buyers before harvesting.
- **Food Rescue:** Helps sell surplus agricultural products and reduce wastage.
- **Product Transparency:** Displays product origin, harvest date, quantity, and quality information.

### Expected Benefits

- Better income opportunities for farmers.
- Affordable and fresh products for consumers.
- Direct connections with bulk buyers.
- Reduced transportation expenses.
- Minimized agricultural wastage.
- Improved supply chain efficiency.

### Our Vision

To build a smarter and more sustainable agricultural ecosystem where farmers receive fair opportunities, consumers get quality products, and technology makes agricultural trade more efficient.

**VERDANT AI – Empowering Farmers. Connecting Markets. Growing Futures.**


## Technical Approach
VERDANT AI follows a user-centric approach to simplify agricultural trading and distribution. The platform brings farmers, buyers, and logistics providers together through a unified marketplace.

It focuses on improving accessibility, transparency, demand awareness, and delivery coordination while providing intelligent insights to support better agricultural decisions.

The approach emphasizes simplicity, reliability, and practical solutions to real-world agricultural challenges.
